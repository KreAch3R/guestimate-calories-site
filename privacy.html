Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Guestimate Calories ("the app") is an Android app that compares a rough daily calorie estimate with the change in your weight. It has no servers, no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The developer does not receive any of your data.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The calorie estimates you type in.
  • Your profile: sex, date of birth, height, activity level and the calorie formula you chose.
  • Weekly results the app calculates from the above.
  • App settings, such as the theme.

This data stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Health Connect

With your permission, the app reads your weight, exercise sessions and heart rate from Android Health Connect. It is read-only: the app never writes to Health Connect. This data is used only on your phone to calculate your results and is not copied anywhere else. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Health Connect.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to back up, the app asks you to sign in with your Google account and to allow access to its own private app-data folder in your Google Drive (the scope drive.appdata). The app uploads one file, guestimate-backup.json, containing your estimates, your profile and your saved weekly results. Weight, workout and heart-rate data from Health Connect are not included.

  • Only this app can see that folder. It does not appear in your Drive file list, and the app cannot see any of your other Drive files.
  • The file goes directly from your phone to your Google account. The developer cannot access it.
  • You can turn on automatic daily backups in the app settings, and turn them off again at any time.
  • You can delete the backup by removing the app's data in your Google account: Google Account → Data & privacy → Third-party apps and services, or Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, other than the backup to your own Google Drive that you choose to turn on.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
