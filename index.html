Guestimate Calories

Guess your calories. Let your scale keep you honest.

Guestimate Calories is an Android app. Each day you type a rough guess of how much you ate. The app reads your weight from Health Connect and works out how many calories your body actually used or stored. At the end of each week it tells you, in plain words, how close your guess was.

How it works

  1. Guess. Type a rough number for the day. No food scanning or weighing.
  2. Weigh. Your scale app shares your weight through Health Connect. The app only reads it.
  3. Compare. The change in your average weight is compared with your estimate. Your guesses never change your calorie target.

Your data

Everything is stored on your phone. The optional Google Drive backup saves a single file to a private folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can open. See the privacy policy for the details.
